About Me

Genetic diagnostics (ICM, Lugo)

Instituto de Estudios Celulares y Moleculares (ICM), Lugo · Aug 2020

Internship in a clinical genetic diagnostics laboratory. I performed DNA/RNA extraction and quantification, and took part in Array CytoSNP (CNV detection) and Array-GSA (SNPs) protocols, interpreting results alongside databases such as ClinVar, Varsome and NCBI to assess their clinical relevance. I also analyzed Sanger sequences and NGS data for exome reports, and assisted with sample collection and diagnostic PCR for COVID-19 and HPV.

As a personal contribution, I designed a bioinformatics filter for the company's analysis software (used for panels such as MiPharma and DPYD): starting from a curated list of clinically relevant SNPs (rs code, change and gene involved), the filter automatically discarded irrelevant variants among the thousands detected in each GSA test, substantially speeding up the analysis.
